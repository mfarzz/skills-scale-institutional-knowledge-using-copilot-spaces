# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors / Business Owners

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors or Business Owners provide organizational sponsorship and ensure the project continues to support its intended business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm sponsorship, strategic alignment, and expected business outcomes
- Help resolve high-impact priority, funding, or resource decisions
- Make or escalate decisions that exceed the project team's authority

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Align with the Product Manager on desired outcomes and priority trade-offs
- Work with the Project Manager on milestone decisions, escalations, and stakeholder alignment
- Provide direction to Stakeholders while leaving day-to-day product and delivery decisions with their accountable owners

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical direction and coordinate engineering decisions and dependencies across the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate architecture and implementation decisions
- Identify technical dependencies, constraints, and risks
- Support engineering estimates and ensure solutions are maintainable and testable

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on design, implementation, and code quality
- Partner with QA/Testing on testability and technical validation
- Inform the Project Manager of technical dependencies and delivery risks, and advise the Product Manager on technical trade-offs

---

## UX / Accessibility Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Accessibility Leads bring user research, interaction design, and accessibility requirements into product planning and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Translate user needs and research into design recommendations
- Define usability and accessibility considerations for features
- Help validate that delivered experiences meet user needs and accessibility expectations

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to align user needs with product outcomes and acceptance criteria
- Work with Developers to apply designs and accessibility requirements during implementation
- Collaborate with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility validation, and share findings with Stakeholders

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Leads advise teams on security, privacy, and data-handling requirements throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, and data protection requirements
- Coordinate risk reviews and recommend mitigations
- Advise on validation and escalation for material security or privacy concerns

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers to incorporate safeguards into design and implementation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on security and privacy validation
- Raise risks through the Project Manager and established security escalation paths, and help the Product Manager understand impacts to scope and outcomes

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads help teams define how project outcomes will be measured and how reliable data will be collected.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurement approaches, metric baselines, and instrumentation needs
- Coordinate data quality and reporting requirements
- Help interpret results after delivery and identify gaps in measurement

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to connect success metrics to product outcomes
- Coordinate with Developers on instrumentation and data collection
- Work with the Project Manager to include measurement dependencies in plans and status reporting, and share findings with Stakeholders

---

## Release / Operations Owners

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Owners coordinate operational readiness and support safe deployment and ongoing service operation.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate operational readiness, deployment planning, and monitoring
- Confirm runbooks, rollback or mitigation plans, and operational handoffs are prepared
- Surface service risks and post-release issues to the delivery team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to confirm release readiness and smoke-test coverage
- Coordinate timing, communications, and dependencies with the Project Manager
- Align with Stakeholders and Customer Support / Success Representatives on release impacts and readiness

---

## Customer Support / Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support / Success Representatives represent customer-facing support needs before and after a release.

### Responsibilities
- Surface common customer needs, feedback, and support risks
- Prepare support teams with relevant guidance and known issues
- Route post-release feedback and recurring issues to the project team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Share customer insights with the Product Manager to inform priorities and outcome evaluation
- Coordinate readiness and customer communications with the Project Manager and Release / Operations Owner
- Work with Stakeholders to ensure support guidance reflects customer and business needs

---
